✨ What's new in 2.0.0

A new app, built on the official Bee Engine SDK. Up to 10 wallets in one install, and taps are automatic. Each signed message is re-sent until it lands, the next session is mined while the current one is delivered, and rewards that are due are claimed before they expire.

It updates itself (Install and restart), keeps mining from the tray when you close the window, and can start with Windows. No telemetry: the log stays on your PC (Save log (.txt)).

On Android it is the same app: wallets, automatic taps and the log (Save log (.txt)) work as on Windows. It mines while the app is open and on screen. Later versions come as a new APK from this page, which installs over 2.0.0.

↻ Coming from goblingames 1.x on Windows?

1. In 1.x press Stop and leave it open for about 15 minutes, so it claims what it has already earned.
2. Run the 2.0.0 installer. It finds 1.x and offers Uninstall before installing: keep that option, and 1.x is removed before 2.0.0 goes into the same folder.
3. Open 2.0.0 and connect your wallets again (QR code, then approve in AN Wallet). It takes a minute, and your NACKL are on the chain.

A wallet mines on one device at a time: connecting it in 2.0.0 stops it in 1.x on every other device, Android included.

↻ Coming from goblingames 1.x on Android?

If you can, press Stop in 1.x and leave it open for about 15 minutes before uninstalling, so it claims what it has already earned.

2.0.0 is a new app and does not replace 1.x. Uninstall 1.x first (Settings → Apps → goblingames → Uninstall), so the two never mine at the same time. Then install 2.0.0, open it and connect your wallets again (Add wallet → Open AN Wallet, approve, and come back to goblingames). It takes a minute, and your NACKL are on the chain.

A wallet mines on one device at a time: connecting it on the phone stops it on your PC, and the other way round.

⚠ "Unrecognized app" / "unknown sources"?

That is normal — the app is not code-signed yet. On Windows: More info → Run anyway. On Android: allow install unknown apps for your browser or Files, then tap the APK. If Play Protect offers to scan it, Install without scanning is normal for apps from outside the Play Store. Same file you downloaded here.
